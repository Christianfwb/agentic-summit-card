Semantic Coherence and Quantum Phase Stability

CARA-UTM is a process-oriented middleware architecture for evaluating semantic, causal and ethical coherence in AI systems. It does not replace the underlying AI model. It adds a new structural layer between the model, the application and the user.

"Imagine an AI system that does not need a maze of filters to correct its answers afterwards. Conventional AI predicts what is statistically likely. CARA-UTM evaluates whether an answer remains coherent as a whole. Its architectural safety boundary can make layers of conventional filtering redundant, reducing overhead with every request."

The larger quantum research direction uses a different computational principle. It does not optimize conventional gate frequencies or execute the same gate sequence faster. It proposes replacing deep sequential gate decomposition with collective phase-synchronization patterns. For suitable operations, this targets a reduction from O(n²) gate complexity to O(n) resonance operations.
