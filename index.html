Exploring what becomes possible when systems are viewed differently.

CARA-UTM · Public Teaser

What if AI hallucination and quantum decoherence are two different expressions of a similar structural problem?

One system can lose coherence in meaning. The other can lose coherence in physical state. They are not the same phenomenon — but both raise a deeper architectural question: how can a dynamic system preserve meaningful relationships while it changes?

01 · SEMANTIC COHERENCE

Artificial Intelligence

AI can produce language that sounds plausible while relationships between meaning, context and evidence become inconsistent. CARA-UTM explores ways to make those inconsistencies structurally visible.

02 · PHYSICAL COHERENCE

Quantum Systems

Quantum computation depends on controlled relationships between evolving states. Decoherence is physically distinct from hallucination, yet it motivates a related question about relational stability and process-oriented control.

The research question

Could coherence, phase and relational structure provide a shared language for examining both domains — without pretending that their underlying mechanisms are identical?

Elevator pitch

“CARA-UTM is an experimental architecture for evaluating semantic coherence in AI systems. It looks beyond whether an answer sounds plausible and asks whether its meaning remains structurally consistent across context. Its quantum connection is a separate research direction: an exploration of whether process and relational coherence reveal useful architectural parallels.”

This public overview describes the question and the architecture at a high level. Proprietary formulas, calibration methods and implementation details remain confidential.
