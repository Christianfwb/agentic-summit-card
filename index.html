CARA-UTM · Architectural Abstract

What if AI hallucination and quantum decoherence are expressions of the same relational breakdown?

One loses coherence in meaning, the other in physical phase. Both raise the core engineering question: How does a dynamic system preserve relational stability under continuous state change?

01 · SEMANTIC COHERENCE

Artificial Intelligence

Statistical next-token generation divorces syntax from grounding. CARA-UTM treats coherence as a structural invariant rather than an afterthought patched by secondary filters.

02 · PHASE COHERENCE

Quantum Systems

Instead of relying purely on gate-decomposition overhead, the model explores phase-synchronization patterns — aiming to collapse sequence depths from $O(n^2)$ down to $O(n)$ resonance states.

The Core Hypothesis

Coherence, phase alignment, and topological boundaries provide an operational bridge connecting informational clarity with physical state stability.

Elevator Pitch

“Conventional AI predicts statistical likelihood; conventional quantum computing stacks deep gate decompositions. CARA-UTM introduces a process-oriented middleware evaluating semantic, causal, and ethical coherence as execution unfolds. In quantum workflows, replacing deep sequential gates with collective phase-synchronization targets an operational shift from $O(n^2)$ gate complexity to $O(n)$ resonance steps.”
